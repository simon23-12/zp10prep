📝 Quiz 1: Text Types & Formats

Teste dein Wissen über verschiedene Textarten

1. What is the main purpose of a diary entry?

  • A) To express personal thoughts and feelings
  • B) To persuade readers
  • C) To inform the public
  • D) To give instructions

2. Which greeting is most appropriate for a formal email?

  • A) Hey!
  • B) Dear Sir or Madam,
  • C) Hi there,
  • D) What's up?

3. A blog entry is typically written in which style?

  • A) Very formal and academic
  • B) Conversational and engaging
  • C) Strictly objective and impersonal
  • D) Using only technical language

4. What should a speech always include?

  • A) A clear introduction, main body, and conclusion
  • B) Only facts and statistics
  • C) Personal diary entries
  • D) Informal abbreviations

5. Which ending is appropriate for a story?

  • A) Yours sincerely,
  • B) Best regards,
  • C) A resolution that ties up the plot
  • D) To be continued...

💬 Quiz 2: Discussion & Comment Writing

Wie gut kennst du dich mit Diskussionen und Kommentaren aus?

1. What is essential in a written discussion?

  • A) Presenting both sides of an argument
  • B) Only stating your opinion
  • C) Using emotional language only
  • D) Avoiding any personal perspective

2. Which phrase introduces a contrasting argument?

  • A) Furthermore,
  • B) In addition,
  • C) However,
  • D) Moreover,

3. A comment should...

  • A) Include your personal opinion with supporting reasons
  • B) Only list facts without opinion
  • C) Be written in first person only
  • D) Avoid giving examples

4. Which is the best way to conclude a discussion?

  • A) Summarize main points and state your position
  • B) Introduce new arguments
  • C) Leave the question unanswered
  • D) Repeat the introduction

5. Linking words like "on the one hand" and "on the other hand" are used to...

  • A) Show contrast between different viewpoints
  • B) Add similar ideas
  • C) Give examples
  • D) Conclude an argument

⚠️ Quiz 3: Typical Mistakes

Vermeide diese typischen Fehler!

1. Which sentence is correct?

  • A) I am going to the cinema yesterday.
  • B) I went to the cinema yesterday.
  • C) I go to the cinema yesterday.
  • D) I goes to the cinema yesterday.

2. Choose the correct form:

  • A) He don't like pizza.
  • B) He doesn't likes pizza.
  • C) He doesn't like pizza.
  • D) He not like pizza.

3. Which is the correct comparative form?

  • A) more better
  • B) better
  • C) more good
  • D) gooder

4. Identify the correct sentence:

  • A) There is many people in the room.
  • B) There are many people in the room.
  • C) There am many people in the room.
  • D) There be many people in the room.

5. Which sentence uses the present perfect correctly?

  • A) I have saw that movie.
  • B) I have seen that movie.
  • C) I has seen that movie.
  • D) I seen that movie.
